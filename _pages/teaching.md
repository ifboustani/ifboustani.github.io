## Molecular Modelling and Synthesis of Nanomaterials

<div class="row mt-3">
  <div class="col-md-8 order-2 order-md-1">
    <p>
      This page introduces my book <em>Molecular Modelling and Synthesis of Nanomaterials: Applications in Carbon- and Boron‑based Nanotechnology</em> (© 2020),
      published in the Springer Series in Materials Science (volume 290). The book brings together fundamental modelling tools and modern experimental
      confirmations to trace how atomic clusters develop into functional nanostructures, with a focus on boron and carbon in one‑, two‑, and three‑dimensional forms.
    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Explores the interplay between theoretical prediction and experimental discovery</li>
      <li>Rich in figures and references</li>
      <li>Discusses the historical evolution of theory in materials science</li>
